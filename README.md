# 👟 Adidas Sales Report Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Adidas sales performance, profitability, and customer demographics across regions, retailers, and sales channels.

## 📌 Domain
Retail & Sales Analytics

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze Adidas sales data and uncover trends in revenue, profit margins, units sold, and customer demographics — enabling data-driven decisions on retail strategy, regional performance, and sales channel optimization.

## 🧰 Tools & Skills
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query (data transformation)
- DAX (measures & calculations)
- Interactive Filters & Slicers
- Drill-through & Cross-report Filtering
- Data Visualization (bar, line, pie, donut, map visuals)

## 📂 Dataset Overview
- **Metrics tracked:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Units Sold, Total Margin
- **Dimensions:** Region, State, Month, Product Category, Retailer, Sales Method, Gender, City, Year (2020–2021)

## 📄 Report Pages

### 1. Sales Report
- **Total Sales:** 182.1M | **Total Profit:** 63.4M | **Units Sold:** 462.3K | **Total Margin:** 525.7
- Sales by Product Category (Apparel, Athletic Footwear, Street Footwear)
- Profit by Sales Method (In-store, Outlet, Online)
- Units Sold by Retailer (West Gear, Foot Locker, Walmart, Sports Direct, Kohl's)
- Sales by Region (West, Northeast, Southeast, South, Midwest)
- Sales by Sales Method trend (In-store → Outlet → Online)
- Filters: Region, Month, Product Category, Retailer, State, Year

### 2. User Analysis
- **Total Sales:** 486.2M
- Top 10 Units Sold by City
- Operating Profit of Gender by Product Category
- Operating Profit by Gender & Total Sales by Gender (donut charts)
- Sales by City (map visual)
- Filter: Gender Type (Men/Women)

## 🔧 Process
1. **Data Preparation** — Imported and cleaned sales data using Power Query.
2. **Data Modeling** — Built relationships between sales, retailer, and user data tables; created DAX measures for sales, profit, and margin.
3. **Dashboard Design** — Designed a two-page report (Sales Report + User Analysis) with consistent branding and interactive filters.
4. **Visualization** — Used bar, line, pie, donut, and map visuals to represent multi-dimensional sales trends.

## 📊 Key Insights
- **West Gear** leads unit sales among retailers at **194K units**, followed by Foot Locker (120K) and Walmart (100K).
- **In-store** sales significantly outperform **Outlet** and **Online** channels.
- The **West region** drives the highest sales at **77M**, while the **Midwest** lags at 7M.
- **Street Footwear (65M)** and **Apparel (61M)** are the top-performing product categories.
- **New York, Charleston,** and **Houston** are the top cities by units sold.

## ✅ Outcome
The dashboard provides a clear, filterable view of sales performance across regions, retailers, and customer segments — supporting retail strategy, inventory planning, and targeted marketing decisions.
